Contact us

Use the support form. You don't need a Google account, and you only share an email address if you want a reply.

Open the support form

To help us fix a problem quickly, include:

  1. Your ScreenCap+ version: open the ▾ menu at the right end of the toolbar and choose About ScreenCap+.
  2. Your error log: ▾ menu > Error log… > Copy to clipboard, then paste it into the form. It stays on your PC until you choose to share it.
  3. What you were doing when it happened.

Common questions

ScreenCap+ says a hotkey is taken by another program

Another app already uses that key combination. Open the ▾ menu > Keyboard shortcuts…, pick a different combination (for example add Shift), and click OK.

Where are my captures saved?

By default in Pictures\ScreenCap. The current folder is shown at the top of the ▾ menu, with Open folder and Change folder… right below it.

I can't see the toolbar

Click the ScreenCap+ icon in the system tray (next to the clock), or right-click it and choose Reset toolbar position. The toolbar then returns to the top-right of your main display.

My marker strokes disappear

Strokes fade after the time on the Fade slider. Turn on Hold to keep them until you press Clear.

How do I undo a stroke?

Right-click while drawing, press Ctrl Z while drawing, click ↶ on the toolbar, or use Ctrl Alt Z from anywhere. Redo is ↷, Ctrl Y, or Ctrl Alt Y.

How do I uninstall?

Windows Settings > Apps, find ScreenCap+ and choose Uninstall. Your captures are never deleted.

Refunds and purchases

Purchases from the Microsoft Store are handled by Microsoft. To ask for a refund, go to your order history at account.microsoft.com and choose Request a refund next to ScreenCap+.
