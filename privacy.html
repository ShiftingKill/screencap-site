ScreenCap+ privacy policy

Last updated 24 November 2026 (error log added) · applies to ScreenCap+ for Windows, including the Microsoft Store version

ScreenCap+ does not collect any data. It has no accounts, analytics, telemetry, crash reporting or ads, and it never connects to the internet.

What stays on your PC

  • Captures. PNG files are saved only to the folder you choose (by default Pictures\ScreenCap). If clipboard copy is on, the last capture is also placed on the Windows clipboard.
  • Settings. Camera positions, layouts, colours and hotkeys are stored in %APPDATA%\ScreenCap\config.json. The Store version keeps them in the app's private storage, which Windows removes when you uninstall.
  • Error log. If something goes wrong, ScreenCap+ writes the error to screencap.log next to its settings. It stays on your PC and is never sent anywhere. You can view, copy or save it from the menu (Error log…) if you choose to include it in a support request.
  • Marker strokes are only kept in memory and disappear when they fade, when you clear them or when you quit.

None of this is sent to us or to anyone else. We have no server to receive it.

Screen content

ScreenCap+ reads the screen only inside a capture frame, and only when you press Snap or a snap hotkey. The image is written to your folder and nowhere else.

Microsoft Store

If you buy ScreenCap+ from the Microsoft Store, Microsoft handles the purchase and licensing under Microsoft's privacy statement. We receive only the aggregate sales and download reports that Partner Center provides to every publisher. They contain no personal information about you.

Children

ScreenCap+ collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If a future version ever needed data, this page would change before that version shipped, and the date above would show it.

Contact

Questions about privacy or anything else: use the support page.
